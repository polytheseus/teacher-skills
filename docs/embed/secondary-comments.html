Free middle and high school report card comment builder

Pick the area and level, tick one or two strengths, add a concern only if there is one, then press Write comment. Press Next student to clear the name, strengths and concern and keep the course, unit and limit. Or paste a comment from ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or your school's comment bank into the box at the bottom. It runs entirely in your browser: nothing you type is sent or stored.

It flags fixed-ability labels ("not a math person", "not academic"), predictions ("will fail", "won't get into college"), grades and percentages, discipline records (detentions, referrals), home and personal circumstances, medical and support-plan details (IEP, 504, EHCP, ADHD, extra time in exams), comparisons with classmates ("best in the class", "bottom set"), labels ("lazy", "bad attitude", "class clown"), vague lines ("could do better", "full potential"), "can't", "always" and "never", teacher jargon (DOK, success criteria, AO3, formative), stock and AI-sounding phrases ("a pleasure to have in class", "showcases"), comments with no real learning or no next step, mixing "you" with the student's name, placeholders, mixed he/she pronouns and curly quotes that some report systems turn into question marks.
