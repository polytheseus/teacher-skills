Free preschool comment builder

Set a level for the two or three areas you want to mention: Strength, On track or Next step. Add one moment you noticed if you have one, then press Write comment. If it runs over your character limit, it drops the optional sentences first. Switch Wording to UK progress check at age 2 for a written summary under the three prime areas. You can edit the result and check it again, or paste a comment from ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or a colleague into the box at the bottom. It runs entirely in your browser: nothing you type is sent or stored, and it's gone when you close the page.

It flags labels ("naughty", "clingy", "bossy"), verdicts ("behind", "can't", "never"), comparisons with other children, diagnosis or support-plan words, family details, jargon families won't know (fine motor, self-regulation, domain names), placeholders left in, mixed he/she pronouns from a copied comment, a missing next step or home tip, stock phrases, your character limit, and curly quotes or dashes that some report systems turn into "?". It's a word check: you know the child, it doesn't.
