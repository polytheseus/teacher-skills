Free sub plan builder

Fill in the class details once, then type one line per block: time | class or subject | task | materials | collect. You can paste rows from a spreadsheet (tab-separated) or a Markdown table from ChatGPT. Press Build sub plan to get the one-page plan with checks, then print it, copy it or download it as text. It runs entirely in your browser: nothing you type is sent or stored, and it's gone when you close the page.

It flags blocks with no time or a vague task ("continue with their work"), anything that needs your own login, tech with no paper fallback, new content, no materials location, nothing to collect, long blocks with no early-finisher task, missing help contact, attendance, seating plan or routines, full names, and sensitive details like diagnoses or family circumstances. It's a word check: it can't tell whether the work is right for your class.
