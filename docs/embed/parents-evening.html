Free parents' evening notes builder

Type one line per student: initials | strength | next step | home step | private note (optional). You can also paste rows from a spreadsheet (tab-separated) or a Markdown table from ChatGPT. Press Build notes to get a script per student with slot times, a follow-up message for each, and a printable sheet. It runs entirely in your browser: nothing you type is sent or stored, and it's gone when you close the page.

It flags general praise with no example, targets like "try harder" or "focus more" (with a better pattern), more than one target, missing home steps, scripts too long for the slot, full names (use initials), and sensitive words such as diagnoses, safeguarding or family circumstances, which belong in a private conversation, not on a sheet or in an AI chat. It's a word check: it can't tell whether a target is the right one for that child.
