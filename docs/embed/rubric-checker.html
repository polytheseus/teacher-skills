Free rubric checker

Paste a rubric and press Check. Put the level names in the first row and one criterion per row after that. It reads a Markdown table (what ChatGPT outputs), rows copied straight from Google Docs, Sheets or Word, or CSV. It runs entirely in your browser, and nothing you paste is sent or stored.

It flags judgment words with nothing a marker can see, amount words with no count, levels that only differ by adverbs, two skills in one row, rows that mark presentation instead of learning, empty or one-word cells, and too many or too few criteria. Then it writes a fix-it prompt with the problems listed. It's a word check, so it can't tell whether your criteria match your objective: that's what the calibration test is for.
