Free report card comment builder for English learners

Choose the student's stage of English, the area that's strongest now and the one to work on next, tick a strength or two and the supports you really use, then press Write comment. Edit the result and check it again, or paste a comment from ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or a colleague into the box at the bottom. It runs entirely in your browser: nothing you type is sent or stored.

It flags deficit wording ("limited English", "language barrier", "no English"), programme labels ("an ESL student"), immigration or legal-status details, where the family is from, what the parents do or don't speak, advice to use English at home, comments on accents, comparisons with native speakers, learning-disability or diagnosis guesses, "shy" and "won't speak" (a quiet stage is normal), test levels and teacher jargon families won't know, idioms that don't translate, sentences over 22 words, placeholders, mixed he/she pronouns and curly quotes that some report systems turn into "?". It's a word check: you know the student, it doesn't.
