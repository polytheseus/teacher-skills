Free math comment builder

Pick the grade band, then a level for the one to three strands you want to mention: Strength, On track or Next step. Add a moment you noticed if you have one, and press Write comment. If it runs over your character limit, it drops the optional sentences first. Edit the result and check it again, or paste a comment from ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or a colleague into the box at the bottom. It runs entirely in your browser: nothing you type is sent or stored.

It flags labels ("lazy", "careless", "sloppy"), fixed-ability lines ("bad at math", "not a math person", "not her strong suit"), guesses at feelings ("math anxiety"), "can't" and "doesn't know", "gives up", sets and comparisons ("bottom set", "than most students"), diagnosis and support-plan words, family comparisons ("just like her dad"), jargon families won't know ("decompose", "regrouping", "standard algorithm", standard codes), stock phrases ("math whiz", "a joy to have in class"), "always" and "never", placeholders, mixed he/she pronouns, math vs maths for the wrong country, a missing next step or home tip, and curly quotes that some report systems turn into "?". It's a word check: you know the student, it doesn't.
