Ordinary prompts give you "a pleasure to have in class". These skills tell the AI what experienced teachers know: lead with a specific strength, back it with evidence from your notes, describe behaviour rather than personality, give one next step a parent can help with, never invent achievements, and vary the wording across the class. It checks every comment against a checklist before showing it to you.

Set it up (no coding)

  1. Download the files. Go to the GitHub page, click the green Code button, then Download ZIP. Unzip it and open the chat folder.
  2. Make your assistant in the app you use:
    • ChatGPT: GPTs → Create → Configure. Paste instructions.txt into Instructions and upload the three .md files under Knowledge. (You need a paid plan to make a GPT. On a free plan, make a ChatGPT Project instead.)
    • Gemini: Gems → New Gem. Paste the instructions and add the files under Knowledge.
    • Claude: Projects → Create project. Paste the instructions and upload the files to Project knowledge.
  3. Ask it something: "Here are my notes for six Year 4 students. Write report comments, 80 words max."

Questions

Is it safe to use with student information?

The skills are designed to work without real student data. They use [Name] or initials, ask you to remove anything sensitive (health, SEN/IEP, family, safeguarding), and refuse to draft messages about safeguarding concerns: they point you to your designated safeguarding lead instead. Always follow your school's AI and data protection policy. Nothing is sent to us. The skills are just instructions that run inside your own ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude account.

Will it write comments that sound like a robot?

The skills ban generic phrases, vary sentence openings across a class, and match your school's style. Paste one comment you like and it will copy that voice. You should still read every comment, because you know the child and it only knows your notes.

UK or US?

Both. Tell it "UK, Year 8, maths" or "US, 3rd grade, math" and it adjusts spelling and terminology (term/semester, marks/grades, head of year, homeroom).

Does it replace my judgment?

No. It drafts and you decide. Suggested grades are marked as suggestions, and sanctions and decisions stay with you and your school.
