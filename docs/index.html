What it's like

Sunday night, thirty report comments due Monday? Give your AI assistant the rules an experienced teacher follows, and get your Sunday back.

You type

J.K. – gd effort, reading up 2 lvls,
picks longer books now. fractions
still shaky. chatty in ind. work.
80 words, UK, Year 4

You get

[Name] has made strong progress in reading this term, moving up two levels and now choosing longer chapter books independently. [Name] works hard in lessons, although chatting during independent tasks sometimes slows progress. In maths, fractions are still tricky. Next term, [Name]'s target is to check fraction answers by drawing a quick model. Practising with pizza or chocolate-bar fractions at home would really help.

72 words · strength, evidence, next step · no labels like "chatty"

Ordinary prompts give you "a pleasure to have in class". These skills tell the AI what experienced teachers know: lead with a specific strength, back it with evidence from your notes, describe behaviour rather than personality, give one next step a parent can help with, never invent achievements, and vary the wording across the class. It checks every comment against a checklist before showing it to you.

The free skills

Set it up (no coding, about 3 minutes)

1. Grab two things: the instructions and the guide (one file).

Download the guide

On a phone? Open the instructions and copy them from there.

2. Make your assistant in the app you use. Each video takes under a minute.

  1. In ChatGPT, click GPTs in the sidebar, then + Create.
  2. Open the Configure tab. Name it Teacher Assistant.
  3. Paste the instructions into Instructions (Ctrl+V, or ⌘V on a Mac).
  4. Under Knowledge, click Upload files and add teacher-assistant-guide.md.
  5. Click Create, choose Only me, and save. It's in your sidebar now.

Making a GPT needs a paid ChatGPT plan. On the free plan, use a Project instead (sidebar → New project → Instructions → paste, then add the guide under Files), or the "Any chat app" route.

  1. In Gemini, open Explore Gems in the sidebar, then New Gem.
  2. Name it Teacher Assistant.
  3. Paste the instructions into Instructions.
  4. Under Knowledge, add teacher-assistant-guide.md.
  5. Click Save. Find it later under Gems.
  1. In Claude, open Projects, click New project and name it Teacher Assistant.
  2. Click Set project instructions, paste the instructions and save.
  3. Under Project knowledge, click + and upload teacher-assistant-guide.md.
  4. Start every chat inside the project.

Works in any AI chat app, including free plans and Microsoft Copilot. No setup, just a little more pasting each time:

  1. Start a new chat and attach teacher-assistant-guide.md (the paperclip or + button).

Questions

Is it safe to use with student information?

The skills are designed to work without real student data. They use [Name] or initials, ask you to remove anything sensitive (health, SEN/IEP, family, safeguarding), and refuse to draft messages about safeguarding concerns: they point you to your designated safeguarding lead instead. Always follow your school's AI and data protection policy. Nothing is sent to us. The skills are just instructions that run inside your own ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude account.

Will it write comments that sound like a robot?

The skills ban generic phrases, vary sentence openings across a class, and match your school's style. Paste one comment you like and it will copy that voice. You should still read every comment, because you know the child and it only knows your notes.

UK or US?

Both. Tell it "UK, Year 8, maths" or "US, 3rd grade, math" and it adjusts spelling and terminology (term/semester, marks/grades, head of year, homeroom).

Does it replace my judgment?

No. It drafts and you decide. Suggested grades are marked as suggestions, and sanctions and decisions stay with you and your school.
